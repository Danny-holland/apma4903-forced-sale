# References on the slides

Open copies are in `papers/` when the author or the preprint server posts one. A journal page that is not posted that way is cited by DOI, and the PDF is not in this folder.

## Copies in `papers/`

- D. Hendricks and D. Wilcox, A reinforcement learning extension of the Almgren–Chriss framework for optimal trade execution, IEEE CIFEr, 2014. [hendricks-wilcox-2014.pdf](papers/hendricks-wilcox-2014.pdf). IEEE holds the copyright on this file, which is why the repository is private.
- B. Ning, F. H. Y. Lin, and S. Jaimungal, Double deep Q-learning for optimal execution, [arXiv:1812.06600](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06600), 2018. Later in *Applied Mathematical Finance*, 2021. [ning-lin-jaimungal-2018.pdf](papers/ning-lin-jaimungal-2018.pdf).
- J. Schulman, F. Wolski, P. Dhariwal, A. Radford, and O. Klimov, Proximal policy optimization algorithms, [arXiv:1707.06347](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347), 2017. [schulman-et-al-2017.pdf](papers/schulman-et-al-2017.pdf).
- T. Haarnoja, A. Zhou, P. Abbeel, and S. Levine, Soft actor-critic, ICML 2018, PMLR 80:1861–1870. [arXiv:1801.01290](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290). [haarnoja-et-al-2018.pdf](papers/haarnoja-et-al-2018.pdf).
- A. A. Obizhaeva and J. Wang, Optimal trading strategy and supply/demand dynamics. The file is NBER working paper w11444, [obizhaeva-wang-nber-w11444.pdf](papers/obizhaeva-wang-nber-w11444.pdf). The published version is *Journal of Financial Markets* 16(1):1–32, 2013, [doi:10.1016/j.finmar.2012.09.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2012.09.001). Proposition 3 is the blocks-and-rate schedule.

## Cited on the slides, PDF not included

- R. Almgren and N. Chriss, Optimal execution of portfolio transactions, *Journal of Risk* 3(2):5–39, 2001. [doi:10.21314/JOR.2001.041](https://doi.org/10.21314/JOR.2001.041). The reserve formulas are their equations (9)–(10), p. 12. Temporary impact, the variance, and the hyperbolic holdings are equations (5), (7), and (17) of the December 2000 working paper, which the article publishes.
- D. Bertsimas and A. W. Lo, Optimal control of execution costs, *Journal of Financial Markets* 1(1):1–50, 1998. [doi:10.1016/S1386-4181(97)00012-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1386-4181(97)00012-8).
- A. F. Perold, The implementation shortfall: paper versus reality, *Journal of Portfolio Management* 14(3):4–9, 1988.
- R. S. Sutton and A. G. Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, 2nd ed., MIT Press, 2018, §1.3. The authors post the book at [incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html). The textbook file on this machine is not in the repository.
- C. J. C. H. Watkins and P. Dayan, Q-learning, *Machine Learning* 8:279–292, 1992. [doi:10.1007/BF00992698](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00992698).
- V. Mnih, K. Kavukcuoglu, D. Silver, et al., Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning, *Nature* 518:529–533, 2015. [doi:10.1038/nature14236](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14236).
